About SWD Store

SWD Store is a student marketplace for discovering and listing pre-owned items within the BITS Pilani community.

How it works

  1. Sign in and create a listing with its category, price, description, and photos.
  2. Browse listings and contact a seller directly when an item interests you.
  3. Manage your own listings and mark items as sold when they are no longer available.

For questions or feedback, use the feedback form in the application.
